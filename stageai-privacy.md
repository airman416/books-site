### Privacy Policy for Staging AI

**Last updated:** December 14, 2025

**1. Introduction**
Welcome to Staging AI ("we," "our," or "us"), operated by **SwipeFeed LLC**. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy notice or our practices with regard to your personal information, please contact us at **swipefeedlive@gmail.com**.

**2. Information We Collect**
We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you use the Staging AI app.
*   **Usage Data:** We collect anonymous data regarding your usage of the app to improve performance and user experience (e.g., button taps, crash logs).
*   **Photos:** We collect the photos you upload solely for the purpose of generating the AI-staged images. These photos are transmitted to our AI providers (Google Gemini) for processing but are not used by us to train public AI models.
*   **Subscription Data:** We use third-party services (RevenueCat, Apple App Store) to process payments. We do not store or have access to your credit card details.

**3. How We Use Your Information**
We use the information we collect to:
*   Provide and operate the Staging AI services.
*   Process your transactions and manage your subscription.
*   Analyze usage patterns to fix bugs and optimize performance.
*   Comply with legal obligations.

**4. Data Storage and Security**
We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures designed to protect the security of any personal information we process. Your generated images and listing data are primarily stored locally on your device using on-device storage to maximize privacy. Images sent to AI providers for processing are handled according to their API data privacy standards.

**5. Third-Party Services**
We may share data with the following third-party vendors for specific purposes:
*   **Google Gemini API:** For generating AI images and text analysis.
*   **RevenueCat:** For managing subscriptions and purchases.
*   **Firebase (Google):** For remote configuration and analytics.
*   **Apple:** For payment processing and app distribution.

**6. Your Privacy Rights**
Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, such as the right to access, correct, or delete your data. To exercise these rights, please contact us at **swipefeedlive@gmail.com**.
